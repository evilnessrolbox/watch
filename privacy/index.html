Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Your audio stays on your device

Audio Studio edits audio inside your web browser. The files you open are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see, store or listen to them. When you close or refresh the page, your audio is gone.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or any files. Like most websites, our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages visited, to keep the site running and secure.

Cookies and advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ad Settings, or learn more about opting out of third-party ad cookies at aboutads.info. You can find out how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party services

To create MP3 files, the page loads an open-source encoder library from the cdnjs content delivery network. That request may reveal your IP address to the CDN provider. Ads are loaded from Google as described above.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at audiomodifiercontact@gmail.com.
